# EchelonFox identity

## Naming

- The name is **EchelonFox** everywhere: wordmark, navigation, page titles, Open Graph text and structured data. The "CONSULTING" descriptor and the "EchelonFox AI Consulting" / "EchelonFox Consulting" names were retired on 14 November 2026.
- Positioning: **a revenue operations consultancy.** Headline: *Find the revenue your business already earned and lost.*
- The Privacy Policy and Terms of Service keep their existing legal entity wording.

## Wordmark

`components/site/Wordmark.tsx` is a text-based mark using the self-hosted Manrope variable font at weight 500, with restrained kerning. No descriptor, illustrated fox, added symbol, gradient or effect.

### Downloadable assets

All paths below are relative to `public/brand/`:

- `echelonfox-navy.svg`: outlined glyphs in #101C2B, transparent background.
- `echelonfox-white.svg`: outlined glyphs in #FFFFFF, transparent background.
- Matching navy and white `.png` fallbacks (996 × 174).
- `ef-monogram.svg`: geometric EF icon.
- `identity.html`: noindex asset review page.
- `echelonfox-consulting-*.svg/png`: the retired wordmark with the CONSULTING descriptor. Kept only so existing external links don't break; nothing on the site uses them.

The SVG wordmarks use outlines, so they don't depend on an installed font. The outlines derive from the locally licensed Manrope font; the OFL licence remains in `app/fonts/`.

## Favicon

The EF monogram uses a navy square and white rectilinear letters, drawn on a 16-unit grid. Assets: `app/icon.svg`, `app/favicon.ico`, `public/favicon-16.png`, `public/favicon-32.png` and `public/apple-touch-icon.png`.
